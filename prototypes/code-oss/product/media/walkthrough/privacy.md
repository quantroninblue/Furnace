# Your data stays yours

- **No AI GUI account and no telemetry.**
- Conversations, saved rules, checkpoints and the activity log are stored on this device only.
- A message goes only to the model you chose, with the context you shared and what the agent reads to do the task.
- Keys and sign-ins live in a separate connection service, and in your system's secure storage only when you choose *Remember*.
- **Copy Diagnostics** and **Send Feedback** contain no conversation text, keys or account names, and nothing is sent until you send it.
